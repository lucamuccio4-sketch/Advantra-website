2. Our two roles

As a controller, we decide why and how personal data is used for our own purposes: our website, enquiries, marketing and managing our client relationships.

As a processor, we handle personal data on behalf of our business clients (for example a roofing or building firm) when we run their AI receptionist, chatbot, WhatsApp automation or CRM. In that case the client is the controller and decides how its customers’ data is used. We act only on the client’s documented instructions under a data processing agreement.

4. WhatsApp conversations and phone numbers

Where we run WhatsApp automation for a client, we process the customer’s WhatsApp phone number, profile name (if shared), message content, media they send, timestamps and delivery status. This is used to answer enquiries, follow up, send updates and appointment reminders, and to log the conversation in the client’s CRM.

Messages are sent over the WhatsApp Business Platform, which is operated by Meta. Meta handles data under its own terms and privacy policy. Automated marketing or follow-up messages are only sent where the client has a valid lawful basis and the customer’s consent, and every customer can reply to opt out at any time.

5. AI processing and call handling

We use AI systems to understand and respond to calls, chats and messages, summarise conversations, qualify enquiries and route information. AI-generated responses may be reviewed by humans, and we do not use AI to make decisions that have legal or similarly significant effects on individuals without human involvement. Where AI voice agents answer calls, callers should be told they are speaking to an automated assistant and, where applicable, that calls may be recorded or transcribed.

We do not allow our AI providers to use client or customer data to train their general models, unless a client has expressly agreed. [CONFIRM against each provider’s settings and contract]

10. Your rights under UK GDPR

You have the right to be informed; to access your data; to have inaccurate data corrected; to have data erased; to restrict or object to processing (including direct marketing); to data portability; to withdraw consent at any time; and to not be subject to solely automated decisions with significant effects.

To use these rights, see our Data Deletion Policy or contact us above. We respond within one month. If you are the customer of one of our clients, we may pass your request to that client, as they are the controller.
