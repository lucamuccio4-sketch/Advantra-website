2. Your right to erasure

Under UK GDPR you can ask us to delete your personal data, for example where it is no longer needed, you withdraw consent, you object to processing and there is no overriding reason to continue, or it was processed unlawfully. The right is not absolute, but we will always tell you if we cannot fully comply and why.

3. How to request deletion

  • Email: [PRIVACY EMAIL ADDRESS] with the subject “Data Deletion Request”
  • Phone: 07933 225525
  • Post: [REGISTERED OFFICE ADDRESS]
  • WhatsApp: if you are messaging a business that uses our automation, you can also reply “STOP” to opt out of messages and tell them you want your data deleted.

Please include your name, the phone number or email address we hold for you, the business you dealt with (if you were contacted through one of our clients) and what you would like deleted.

4. What happens next

  1. Acknowledgement: we confirm receipt promptly.
  2. Identity check: we may ask for reasonable information to confirm it is you. We will not ask for more than we need.
  3. Assessment: we locate your data across our systems and our providers, and decide whether we are the controller or processor.
  4. Action: we delete the data, or pass the request to the relevant client if they are the controller. We aim to complete requests within one month, extendable by up to two further months for complex requests, in which case we will tell you.
  5. Confirmation: we confirm what was deleted and anything we had to keep.

5. What we delete

  • Contact details, WhatsApp phone numbers and profile names
  • WhatsApp conversation history, call recordings and transcripts
  • CRM records and enquiry forms
  • Copies held by our AI and other service providers [CONFIRM how you make sure providers delete data]
